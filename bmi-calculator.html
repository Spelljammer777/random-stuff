BMI Index Calculator

Body mass index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight that applies to adult men and women. This is an advanced BMI index calculator in that it not only computes standard BMI, but it computes an Adjusted BMI as well. The Adjusted BMI takes into account age, muscularity, and body frame as well as the typical gender, weight, and height.

Healthy Living & Resources

Maintaining a healthy weight is important for overall well-being. A balanced diet and regular physical activity are key components of a healthy lifestyle.

Healthy Eating Tips:

  • Focus on whole foods: fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats.
  • Limit processed foods, sugary drinks, and excessive saturated/trans fats.
  • Control portion sizes.
  • Stay hydrated by drinking plenty of water.
  • Listen to your body's hunger and fullness cues.
